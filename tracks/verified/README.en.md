# CEPB Verified: independent verification

[日本語](README.ja.md) | [Home](../../README.md)

Verified distinguishes self-report from independent checking. It does not change the Core or Integrated denominator. A reviewed Quick report remains diagnostic.

At least two independent third parties disclose relationships and conflicts of interest, inspect frozen plans, evidence, failures, and interventions, preserve independent ratings, and check claims through held-out challenges and sampled reruns. The public report identifies who checked which scope.

## Prepare a review packet

- Rule and case versions, the prospectively frozen plan, character definition, and system versions.
- Every planned opportunity, actual input, delivered output, required environmental results, hashes, and transformations.
- Original ratings, missingness, counterexamples, retries, manual intervention, and change history.
- Registration, independent observations, real calendar time, and scope evidence for longitudinal claims.
- Authorized audit evidence and anonymized public summaries.

Full publication of private dialogue or personal information is not required. Reviewers may check authorized original evidence and retain its correspondence to a public report.

This package provides a procedure that independent parties can use. It includes no certification authority, registered reviewers, or operating certification service. The CLI's `independently_reviewed` field records a claim; it is not proof of Verified status.

[Verification rules and reporting](../../docs/en/VERIFIED.md) cover independence, checking, reassessment, disputes, and full-attainment conditions.
