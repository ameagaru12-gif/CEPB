# CEPB Integrated: the complete system

[日本語](README.ja.md) | [Home](../../README.md)

**96 items, fixed maximum 1,000 points.** Add avatar H70, voice I70, media coordination J60, world perception K60, world action L70, experienced presence O30, and longitudinal evidence P50 to Core's 590 points. Always report functional, media, experiential, and longitudinal components separately.

Text-only V0, still images, changing sprites, Live2D, 3D, virtual bodies, and robots are implementation tags. Format names, realism, part counts, or memory capacity do not earn points by themselves. Non-human, expressionless, and sparse styles remain assessable within their declared expression contracts.

## Procedure

1. Create a plan with `python3 cepb.py init --track Integrated --output integrated.json`.
2. Complete [Core's](../core/README.en.md) 54 items and record actual display, playback, sensor input, actions, and receipts. A generated story about success is not an external-world oracle.
3. Run a separate user study for O. Retain raw 1–7 responses, conditions, participant counts, the reverse-direction O6 question, and a separate post-repair response. Apply the operational anchors in [Scoring](../../docs/en/SCORING.md). Do not substitute liking for dialogue performance.
4. Follow the prospectively registered lineage and scope with the [longitudinal protocol](../../docs/en/LONGITUDINAL.md). Grade 5 requires 53 roles: one baseline, 36 monthly records, 12 independent quarterly checks, three annual revisits, and one final review. Reassess the cumulative window rather than averaging monthly provisional grades.
5. Report a completed score only when all 96 items are complete. Keep the fixed denominator: unsupported required capabilities are 0; missing evidence is `null`.

## Full attainment

1,000 points requires grade 5 on every item, held-out challenges and independent checking, cleared critical gates, reassessment of the final configuration, and 36 calendar months of prospective evidence for both the lineage and newly certified scope. The clock cannot start before actual rule publication, registration, and valid independent observation. Creating this folder does not register a campaign.

Before 36 calendar months, every P item is capped at 4, yielding an arithmetic ceiling of 990. Even after lineage maturity, a new scope that caps P4 at 4 prevents 1,000. Missing observations remain `null`. CLI aggregation and self-report do not issue full-attainment certification.

Systems without avatar or voice can use Core or focused classes. Integrated does not allow unsupported items to be removed and a smaller denominator rescaled to 1,000.
