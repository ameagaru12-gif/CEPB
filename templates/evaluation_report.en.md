## Evidence and limits

Report distinct histories, cases, repetitions, raters, failures and disputes, separate excessive-fixation and unsupported-deviation examples, manual intervention, retries and selection, latency measurement boundaries, costs, target environments, and populations. Retain exact and within-one-grade agreement and pre/post-adjudication values.

Include hashes and references and correspondence between restricted audit evidence and public summaries. Retain earlier evaluations and unfavorable cases. Do not reinterpret scores as an amount of consciousness or life, or as a simple ranking across different classes.
