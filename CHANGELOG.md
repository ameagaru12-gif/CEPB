## Migration from the earlier draft / 旧案からの移行

The earlier draft's weights and item identifiers are preserved. The public 1.0.0 protocol adds fixed operational minimums and executable checks that were not completed in the draft. Draft or pilot results, raw 1–7 responses, and old local averages do not become official 1.0.0 scores automatically. Re-evaluate under a frozen 1.0.0 campaign and retain the original report.
