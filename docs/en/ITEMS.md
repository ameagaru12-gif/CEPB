# 96 items and anchors

CEPB-1.0.0 / CEPB-SUITE-1.0.0

0–1 anchors illustrate failures, 3 illustrates ordinary establishment, and 5 requires additional evidence. Rate targets, evidence, and permitted scope rather than one correct line. Public cases or self-review alone do not establish 5.

Initial weights divide each domain equally among six items. Quick reports local candidates for its fixed 18 items and has no completed class score. Core 590, Voice 660, Avatar 660, Multimodal 790, World 720, and Integrated 1000. Do not directly rank scores from different classes.

O uses original human responses assessed under the explicit operational rubric in [SCORING](SCORING.md) §6; thresholds are not empirically validated. Do not silently convert raw responses. O6 requires all planned ratings for both breakdown and repair. P rates actual-UTC prospective cumulative windows and is capped at 4 before thirty-six calendar months (no automatic 4). Newly certified scopes also require thirty-six months for P4.

For A–N, all twelve observations assigned across at least two histories form one item evaluation bundle. Only when multiple complete bundles are planned, average every planned bundle equally. SelfReported formal 0–4 may use one rater; grade 5 and Verified require at least two independent human raters. Quick’s standard local cap is 3, but local candidates of 4 or 5 may be preserved with additional evidence for those levels.

Corresponding development cards are in [CASEBOOK](CASEBOOK.md); the machine-readable source is [data/items.json](../../data/items.json).
